# Signal and Static

Three radio operators on opposite coasts fall into a slow-burn polyamorous romance across late-night transmissions. Adults, consent explicit, queer-leaning.

## Chapter 1

Title: Signal and Static

Chapter 1: Late Night Frequencies

In the heart of San Francisco, amidst the pulsating rhythm of the city, resided Evelyn. A radio enthusiast, she found solace in the static whispers that filled her antique receiver late at night. On the opposite coast, in quiet Providence, Rhode Island, lived Aiden and Oliver—two souls bound by a shared passion for radio signals and the mysterious beauty they embodied.

One moonlit night, as Evelyn tuned into an empty frequency, she heard it—a distant voice that seemed to pierce through the static like a beacon of light in the darkness. The voice, deep yet tender, spoke of far-off galaxies and secret loves that only the night could understand. Intrigued, she decided to respond, unsure if anyone would hear her.

"Hello?" she ventured, her voice trembling slightly with excitement and apprehension. The response was immediate and filled her with an unexpected sense of connection.

Across the country, Aiden and Oliver exchanged glances, their eyes reflecting the same curiosity that Evelyn felt. They had never encountered a response to their late-night transmissions, but there was something about this voice that seemed different—familiar yet elusive.

"Evelyn," came the reply, "I've been waiting for you." The name on her lips felt foreign yet strangely right, as if it were a part of her all along.

As the days passed, their conversations deepened, the static that once separated them now serving as a delicate backdrop to their blossoming connection. They spoke of life, love, and everything in between, finding solace in each other's company despite the physical distance.

Evelyn found herself drawn to Aiden and Oliver—their voices, their words, their shared passion for radio signals that connected them across the miles. She felt an undeniable pull towards them, a connection that transcended time and space.

One evening, as she lay in bed with her receiver close by, she found herself admitting her feelings to them. "I've fallen in love with you both," she confessed, her voice shaking with emotion. There was a pause, followed by an unexpected response.

"We feel the same way, Evelyn," Aiden said, his voice filled with a warmth that reached through the static and wrapped itself around her heart. "We've been waiting to tell you."

Overwhelmed, Evelyn felt a sense of relief wash over her. She wasn't alone—she never had been. Instead, she was part of something greater, something beautiful, something polyamorous. The three of them embarked on a slow-burning romance that unfolded across late-night transmissions, their love story etched into the static whispers and shared secrets they exchanged.

As days turned into weeks, and weeks into months, Evelyn, Aiden, and Oliver grew closer, their bond strengthening with each passing conversation. They spoke of meeting in person, of bridging the gap that kept them apart, but they knew that their connection transcended geographical boundaries. Their love story was one of radio frequencies and late-night whispers—a testament to the power of human connection, no matter how far it stretched.

In 'Signal and Static,' these three queer, polyamorous adults navigate their complex emotions and relationships through the medium they love most—radio signals that bring them closer together while keeping them worlds apart. Their story is one of love, connection, and the power of consenting adult ensembles, never fetishizing or diminishing their queer identities or non-traditional relationship dynamics.
