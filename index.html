How I work

  1. 01
    Truth before theater

    I label prototypes honestly, show what is operational, and avoid inflating roadmap ideas into shipped capabilities.

  2. 02
    Agency by design

    Permission boundaries, local-first options, exportability, and human approval are product features, not legal footnotes.

  3. 03
    Receipts over claims

    Tests, merged pull requests, live demos, architecture diagrams, and reproducible setup instructions carry the story.

  4. 04
    Memorable, then usable

    A product can have soul without sacrificing clarity, accessibility, or operational discipline.
