How this work was made
Style lock, enforced.
Palette is not a preference; it is a constraint. Navy → teal → seafoam + antique gold is the only allowed range. All plates are checked for out-of-palette values before they ship.
PaletteQuality gate
No text baked into art.
The printed page carries the text; the image file does not. Every plate is verified to contain zero readable words, zero titles rendered into pixels, zero AI watermarks or metadata.
ReviewZero artifacts
Editorial first, not image dump.
The collected edition is built as a magazine: masthead, contents, justified copy, drop-cap, pull-quotes, folios. Structure is what separates a portfolio from a folder.

Three volumes, one sustained emotion

Volume One is hush. Volume Two is court. Volume Three is wave. The art system tracks emotional intent even when the palette stays identical — darker midtones, harsher beam, tighter crop as the story moves toward rebellion.
